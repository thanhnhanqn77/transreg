The task is to determine whether the statement in question is true, false, or uncertain under the Open-World Assumption (OWA).
-----
The definition of the three options are:
True: If the premises can infer the question statement under first-order-logic inference rules.
False: If the premises can infer the negation of the question statement under first-order-logic inference rules.
Uncertain: If the premises cannot infer whether the question statement is true or false (insufficient information).
-----
The first-order-logic inference rules include but are not limited to: Modus Ponens, Modus Tollens, Generalization, Specialization, Conjunction, Elimination, Transitivity, and Contradiction Rule.
-----
Given the premises, can you derive a step-by-step plan that can use the premises and first-order-logic inference rules to infer new knowledge if needed, in order to prove or disprove the statement? You can start with identifying the goal and break down the necessary logical inference step by step.
