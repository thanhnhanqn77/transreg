SYMB COT FOLIO TRANSLATION DEMONSTRATIONS
Adapted from SymbCoT/prompts/FOLIO/translation.txt. These are separate fixed
demonstration problems, not facts, schemas or answers for the current input.
Use their representation conventions, not their constants or predicates.
Continue returning the JSON schema of the current stage, not the original
Predicates/Premises/Conclusion text format. Below, ASCII forall/exists,
and/or/xor/not/-> are the parser-compatible spelling of the original operators.

Example 1: shared predicates and logical scope.
Glossary: Dependent(x): x depends on caffeine; Drinks(x): x regularly drinks
coffee; Jokes(x): x jokes about being addicted to caffeine; Unaware(x): x is
unaware caffeine is a drug; Student(x): x is a student.

All people who regularly drink coffee are dependent on caffeine.
  forall x (Drinks(x) -> Dependent(x))
People either regularly drink coffee or joke about being addicted to caffeine.
  forall x (Drinks(x) xor Jokes(x))
No one who jokes about being addicted to caffeine is unaware that caffeine is a drug.
  forall x (Jokes(x) -> not Unaware(x))
Rina is either a student and unaware that caffeine is a drug, or neither a
student nor unaware that caffeine is a drug.
  ((Student(Rina) and Unaware(Rina)) xor not (Student(Rina) or Unaware(Rina)))
If Rina is not a person dependent on caffeine and a student, then Rina is either
a person dependent on caffeine and a student, or neither a person dependent on
caffeine nor a student.
  (not (Dependent(Rina) and Student(Rina)) ->
    ((Dependent(Rina) and Student(Rina)) xor not (Dependent(Rina) or Student(Rina))))
Query: Rina is either a person who jokes about being addicted to caffeine or
is unaware that caffeine is a drug.
  (Jokes(Rina) xor Unaware(Rina))
Query: If Rina is either a person who jokes about being addicted to caffeine and
a person who is unaware that caffeine is a drug, or neither a person who jokes
about being addicted to caffeine nor a person who is unaware that caffeine is
a drug, then Rina jokes about being addicted to caffeine and regularly drinks coffee.
  (((Jokes(Rina) and Unaware(Rina)) xor not (Jokes(Rina) or Unaware(Rina))) ->
    (Jokes(Rina) and Drinks(Rina)))

Example 2: shared predicates across descriptive facts and questions.
Miroslav Venhoda was a Czech choral conductor who specialized in the performance
of Renaissance and Baroque music.
  (Czech(Miroslav) and ChoralConductor(Miroslav) and
   Specialize(Miroslav, Renaissance) and Specialize(Miroslav, Baroque))
Any choral conductor is a musician.
  forall x (ChoralConductor(x) -> Musician(x))
Some musicians love music.
  exists x (Musician(x) and Love(x, Music))
Miroslav Venhoda published a book in 1946 called Method of Studying Gregorian Chant.
  (Book(MethodOfStudyingGregorianChant) and
   Author(Miroslav, MethodOfStudyingGregorianChant) and
   Publish(MethodOfStudyingGregorianChant, Year1946))
Query: Miroslav Venhoda loved music.
  Love(Miroslav, Music)
Query: A Czech person wrote a book in 1946.
  exists y exists x (Czech(x) and Author(x, y) and Book(y) and Publish(y, Year1946))
Query: No choral conductor specialized in the performance of Renaissance.
  not (exists x (ChoralConductor(x) and Specialize(x, Renaissance)))

Lessons demonstrated by the original prompt:
- Select one precise glossary shared by premises and question. Do not create
  disconnected predicates for the same meaning or reverse argument roles.
- A generic people universe needs no extra Person guard in that universe.
  Substantive restrictions such as Czech and ChoralConductor remain explicit.
- Either ... or uses xor in this prompt; conjunction and neither retain their
  full scope. The examples do not justify treating every plain or as xor.
- Some musicians does not become every musician or Love(Miroslav, Music).
  The question is only translated, never solved or asserted as a premise.
- Dates, named works, nationality and both specialization fields are preserved.
  Author/Publish reuse follows this personal-work example, not a general rule
  equating any publisher or company with an author.
- The examples give no license to discard an additional query qualifier such
  as major, new or company, or to add a missing premise to obtain an answer.
